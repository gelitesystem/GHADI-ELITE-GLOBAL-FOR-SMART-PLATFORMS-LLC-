About SUN Platform

One Platform. Every Opportunity.

SUN Smart Platform is a subsidiary of GHADI Elite Global for Smart Platforms LLC, headquartered in Sharjah Media City Free Zone, UAE. Under a robust legal umbrella, SUN operates as a fully-licensed integrated digital platform designed to redefine marketing, events, and competitions across the Middle East, North Africa, and global markets.

Our license covers advertising, digital marketing, promotional competitions, exhibition organization, strategic consulting, and e-commerce — giving partners and clients a single, trusted gateway to reach their audiences at scale.
